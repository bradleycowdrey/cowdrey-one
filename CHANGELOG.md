# What’s new in Cowdrey One

## 1.0.0

The first version: every app by Bradley Cowdrey in one place. Free and open source under the MIT License, like
every app it lists.

- **Discover:** Bearings, Two Degrees, Meridian, Cowdrey.MD, Cowdrey.PDF and Golden Hour, each on its own sky
  with a glimpse of the app, and every app in one list.
- **The right download for this device:** App Store, TestFlight, Mac App Store, `.dmg`, APK, or the web app.
- **Get Every App,** with a ring that counts what’s on this device, and one button for the rest:
  Install All in the Mac and Android apps, Get All (App Store pages in turn) in the iPhone app, and
  Download All in a browser on a Mac or Android phone.
- **The Mac and Android apps see what’s installed,** with versions and an Open button.
- **Library,** with updates for downloaded apps from each app’s GitHub releases.
- **Get on Your Phone:** a QR code, Share and Copy Link, and how to keep Cowdrey One on each device.
- **Starts at the top:** opening Cowdrey One, or switching back to it, shows the top of the screen.
- **The App Store’s pictures:** each app’s card in Discover shows its first App Store screenshot, and its page
  opens on all of them in a row you swipe, as on the App Store.
- **App pages stay put:** a page can no longer slide sideways on a phone, and What’s New shows the words about the
  app without download tables, links and checksums.
- **Only what’s really installed:** an app shows as installed (with Open) only when Cowdrey One sees it on the
  device, never because Get was tapped. The iPhone app sees apps with their own link, such as Two Degrees.
- Light and dark, a floating tab bar on phones with Cowdrey One at the top, and a sidebar on the Mac.
